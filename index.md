# SmartDocs – Smart Documents Writer

SmartDocs by [Studio Pro](https://prostudio.my) is an online documents writer for creating and managing business documents.

## What you can do
- Create and manage invoices, sales receipts, delivery notes, quotations, purchase orders, credit notes, and proforma invoices
- Manage customers and recurring documents
- View reports and currency tools
- Configure settings for your business documents
